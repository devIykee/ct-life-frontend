Real money. You can lose all of it.

This launches a real token on Solana with real money. Token prices can go to zero. Don't launch or buy unless you're prepared to lose all the money you put in. Nothing here is financial advice, and no profit is promised.

01What the Factory does

  • The Factory, also called the Rugpull Factory, lets you create a real token on the Solana blockchain from inside CT Life.
  • Unlike everything else in CT Life, this is not play money. Launching costs real SOL, and buying or selling a token uses real SOL.
  • Tokens launch on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve, a public program on Solana run by Meteora, not by CT Life. In the game, the launch desk carries the Pump.fun name as parody. Launches do not use Pump.fun, and CT Life is not affiliated with Pump.fun or Meteora.
  • You must be 18 or older, and you must accept a risk warning before every launch. You can launch up to 3 tokens in any 24 hours.

02How a launch works

  1. You choose a name, a ticker, an image and an optional description. We check them against our content rules.
  2. We prepare the launch transaction. You review and sign it in your own wallet. Your wallet pays, and the token's creator is your wallet. We never hold your keys or your funds.
  3. The token is created with a fixed supply of 1 billion. Its name, ticker, image and description can never be changed, and they are published permanently to IPFS, a public file network.
  4. The token trades on a bonding curve: the price rises as people buy and falls as they sell. Anyone, inside or outside CT Life, can trade it.
  5. If enough SOL is paid into the curve, the token graduates to a regular Meteora pool. The pool's liquidity is then locked permanently, and nobody can withdraw it.

03Costs and fees

  • To launch: you pay the Solana network fees and the deposits needed to create the token and its pool. The app shows an estimate before you sign. CT Life charges no launch fee.
  • On every trade: a trading fee of 1% is taken from each buy and sell on the bonding curve. Meteora keeps 20% of that fee. The rest is split equally between CT Life and the token's creator.
  • After graduation: the regular pool charges its own 1% trading fee. The locked liquidity is held half by CT Life and half by the creator, and each earns that share of the pool's fees.
  • CT Life earns money when tokens launched through the Factory are traded. We earn whether the price goes up or down.
  • Network fees are paid even if a transaction fails, and they are never refunded.

04The risks

  • Total loss. A token can lose all of its value at any time, even minutes after it launches. Assume any token you buy can go to zero.
  • Rug pulls. A creator can sell their own tokens at any time and crash the price. CT Life may label the token and penalise the creator's character in the game, but it cannot stop the sale or get your money back.
  • No buyers. You may not be able to sell at the price you see, or at all.
  • Fast, volatile prices. Prices on a bonding curve can move sharply with a single trade.
  • Scams and copies. Anyone can launch a token with any name. A token that looks official, or uses a famous name, may be a scam.
  • Final and public. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed or cancelled, and everything you do on-chain is public, including your wallet address.
  • Technology. Meteora's programs, the Solana network and your wallet can have bugs, outages or attacks. Transactions can fail or be delayed. If you lose your wallet's keys, nobody can recover your tokens.
  • No protection. These tokens are not regulated or insured, and no compensation scheme covers them.
  • The game is not advice. Feed posts, characters' calls, rankings, prices and hype inside CT Life are part of the game. They are not recommendations to buy or sell anything.
  • Law and tax. Laws on tokens are changing and differ by country. A token, or what you say about it, could break the law where you or your buyers live. You may owe tax on trades.

05If you launch a token

  • You are the token's creator, not CT Life. You are responsible for the token and everything you say about it.
  • Do not promise profits, returns, dividends or future plans you cannot keep. Doing so may make your token a regulated investment and may be illegal.
  • Do not impersonate any person, project, company or brand, and do not use names, tickers, images or descriptions that are offensive, misleading or banned by the Terms.
  • If you sell or move a large share of your own token, CT Life may mark it as a rug, post about it in the game's feed and apply in-game penalties to your character.
  • We may hide your token inside CT Life at any time. Nobody, including us, can delete it from the blockchain or IPFS.

06If you buy a token

  • Only spend money you can afford to lose completely.
  • Tokens are made by other players, not by CT Life. We do not review, approve or vouch for any token, and a token being shown in CT Life is not an endorsement.
  • Check the token's address before you buy. Names and tickers can be copied.

07What CT Life does and does not do

  • We provide the screens and prepare the transactions. You sign them in your own wallet, and Meteora's programs on Solana carry them out.
  • We do not hold your crypto, sell tokens to you, give investment advice or promise any price or profit.
  • We are not responsible for losses from token prices, other users' tokens, Meteora's programs, the Solana network or your wallet. See the Terms of Use for the full terms.

08Your local laws

The Factory is not blocked in any country. It is your responsibility to follow the laws where you live, including laws on securities, consumer protection, tax and sanctions. Do not use the Factory where it is illegal for you, or if you are under 18.
