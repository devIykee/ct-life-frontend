Play money. No cash value. 18+

The Rug Room is not real-money gambling. You play with CT Life Cash, which can never be withdrawn or cashed out, and you can only ever win more Cash.

01Play money only

  • Every game in the Rug Room uses CT Life Cash, the game's play money. No crypto, tokens or real money can be staked.
  • Cash has no real-world value. It can never be withdrawn, cashed out, redeemed, or swapped for money, crypto, NFTs or anything else of value.
  • Cash cannot be sent to other players, so it cannot be passed on or sold.
  • You can buy Cash with crypto. Buying Cash only gives you more play money for the game. It is never a deposit, and it never comes back out.
  • The game shows Cash amounts with a $ sign. That is only how Cash is displayed. It is not dollars.

02No real prizes

  • Winnings are paid in Cash only.
  • The Rug Room never pays out money, crypto, tokens, NFTs or anything that can be traded.
  • Winning at play-money games does not mean you would win at real-money gambling. CT Life does not promote, advertise or link to real-money gambling.

03The games

  • Slots: three reels.
  • Crash: cash out before the line crashes.
  • Coinflip: call heads or tails against the house. A win pays 1.9 times your stake.
  • Head-to-head coinflip: flip against another player for bragging rights only. Nothing is staked, and no Cash changes hands.

Each bet is between $1.00 and $100.00 in Cash. Over many rounds, every game pays back about 95% of what is staked. That means that over time, you lose Cash.

04Provably fair

  1. Before each round, our server picks a secret random number and shows you its fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash).
  2. The result comes only from that secret number. Nothing you or we do after that can change it.
  3. After the round, the server reveals the secret number, so you can check that it matches the fingerprint and gives the same result.

Your past rounds and their numbers are listed under Fairness in the Rug Room, where you can check any round on your phone.

05Daily limits

  • Stake limit: you can stake up to $500.00 in Cash per day.
  • Loss limit: you can lose up to $250.00 in Cash per day.
  • Both limits reset at midnight UTC. You can see what is left in the Rug Room under Limits.
  • Break reminder: after 30 minutes of continuous play, the game suggests a break.

06Take a break (self-exclusion)

  • In the Rug Room, open Limits and choose Take a break from the Rug Room. You can lock yourself out for 1 to 365 days.
  • During the break you cannot place any bet, in any Rug Room game.
  • You can make a break longer, but you cannot shorten it or end it early.

07Adults only

You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher, to play in the Rug Room. The Rug Room checks your age confirmation before every bet.

08If gaming stops being fun

  • United States: National Problem Gambling Helpline, call 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), or visit ncpgambling.org.
  • United Kingdom: National Gambling Helpline, call 0808 8020 133, or visit gambleaware.org. To block real-money gambling sites, see gamstop.co.uk.
  • Everywhere else: Gambling Therapy offers free support at gamblingtherapy.org.
