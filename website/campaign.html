What is CT Life?

CT Life is a 3D life simulation game inspired by Crypto Twitter. Make a character, meet people, work short jobs, trade fictional coins and build projects with friends.

Can I play it now?

CT Life is being built for Solana Seeker. There is no public download yet. The Get the game button shows the current availability.

Is this real-money trading?

The game’s markets use fictional coins and in-game cash. Game cash cannot be withdrawn. Any real-wallet purchases are separate from the game’s trading outcomes.

What can I do with friends?

CT Life is designed for multiplayer. Meet around the city, hang out in venues and build projects together.

Do I need a Seeker?

CT Life is being built as a native Android game for Solana Seeker, with wallet sign-in. This website introduces the game; it isn’t a browser version.
