Art & credits.

Our website brings the world of CT Life to life through original campaign illustrations and captures from the game in development.

Campaign illustrations

The character ensemble and rooftop scene were created for CT Life with OpenAI image generation, then reviewed and exported for this website. The city illustration is existing original generated CT Life concept art. These images express an art direction. They are not screenshots, playable character models or evidence of completed game features.

Inside the current build

The game viewer contains captures from CT Life development builds on Solana Seeker, dated 7 October 2026. They show the café, phone timeline and Clip Cut job using local development fixtures and sample data. They do not demonstrate connected multiplayer or live payments. The captures retain their development labels and are compressed for delivery without changing their contents.

Website interactions

The city guide is an illustrated introduction. The personality cards are a playful website interaction; they do not create a game account or save a character. Copying a bio writes that text to your clipboard only when you press the copy button.

This website has no analytics, sign-up form, wallet connection or payment processing. All fonts and images load locally. CT Life has no public download yet.
