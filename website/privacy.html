The short version

  • You sign in with a Solana wallet. We never get your private keys or seed phrase.
  • We keep what the game needs to run: your wallet address, your character, your game progress, your messages and your purchases.
  • We do not sell your data, and there are no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • We do not record voice or video. We do not store your real-world location.
  • Anything on the blockchain, including tokens you launch in the Factory, is public and permanent. Nobody can delete it, including us.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. See what deletion removes and what it does not.

01Who we are

  • CT Life is CT Life, a fun project built with love by Solana devs ("we", "us"). We are responsible for your personal data under this policy.
  • This policy covers the CT Life Android app, the ctlife.fun website and our servers. Questions go to immadominion@gmail.com.

03Real token launches (the Factory)

When you launch a token in the Factory, it is a real token on the Solana blockchain. See the Token launch risk disclosure.

  • What we keep: a record of each launch: the wallet that launched it, the token's address and pool, its name, ticker, description and image, and its status. We also keep the buys and sells of tokens launched through CT Life, read from the public blockchain, so we can show prices, charts and volume.
  • The token's image and details are published permanently. We resize the image and remove its hidden data (such as location tags), then publish the image and the token's name, ticker and description to IPFS, a public file network, through our provider Pinata. Once published, they cannot be changed or removed, by us or anyone else.
  • Watching for sales: we ask our blockchain provider, Helius, to tell us about transactions from your launching wallet and your token's pool. We use this to show trades and to spot when a creator sells a large share of their own token (see the Terms).

04Location (event check-in only)

  • CT Life only uses your phone's location if you choose to check in at a real event, such as Breakpoint 2026. We explain why before your phone asks for permission.
  • When you tap Check in, your phone sends its position, its accuracy and whether the location is faked, once. Our server checks whether you are at the venue and then throws the position away. It is never stored or logged.
  • We keep only that you checked in, when, and whether you chose to appear on the public attendee list. You are hidden from that list unless you turn it on.
  • We never track your location in the background.

05Game statistics and server logs

Game statistics

  • To balance the game, our own servers record key game events: signing in, creating a character, claiming daily rewards and quests, finishing work shifts, Casino rounds and shop orders.
  • Each event is linked only to your internal account number. It never includes your wallet address, alias, IP address, device ID, location or message text.
  • These records stay on our own database, are not shared, and are deleted after 180 days.

Server logs and IP addresses

  • Our servers log each request: the time, the address requested and the result. We shorten your IP address before it is logged (the last part is removed), and we do not log message content, query strings or location.
  • To limit sign-in attempts, we keep your full IP address in memory for 60 seconds. No database table stores IP addresses.
  • Our hosting provider may keep its own access logs, which can include full IP addresses, for as long as that provider keeps them.

06What we do not collect

  • Your private keys or seed phrase. We will never ask for them.
  • Your advertising ID.
  • Data for advertising tools, third-party analytics tools or crash-reporting tools. The app has none.
  • Your contacts, text messages or photos you have not chosen to upload.
  • Your location in the background, or at any time other than an event check-in you start.
  • Recordings of voice or video rooms.

07Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide CT Life to you (our contract with you): your account, gameplay, messages, voice rooms, purchases and token launches.
  • For our legitimate interests: security, preventing fraud and multiple accounts, moderation, fixing bugs and balancing the game.
  • With your consent: notifications, microphone and camera, the event check-in and the attendee list. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • To meet legal duties: for example, keeping payment records.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

09Public and on-chain data

  • Your wallet address and every blockchain transaction you make, including purchases and token launches, are public on the Solana blockchain. Anyone can see them.
  • We do not control the blockchain and cannot change or delete anything on it. The same is true of token images and details published to IPFS.

11Deleting your account

  • In the app, open your phone, then Settings, then Delete account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.
  • We erase your wallet link, character, profile, notification tokens, follows, blocks and uploaded files. Direct messages and feed posts you wrote are replaced with "[deleted]". Records we must keep are kept under an anonymous ID.
  • Some data is not removed yet, including group chat messages you wrote. Read the full list before you delete.

12Your rights

  • Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to restrict or object to how we use it. Email immadominion@gmail.com. We may ask you to prove you control the wallet linked to the account.
  • You can turn off notifications, the attendee list, and microphone, camera or location access at any time.
  • You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the authority where you live.

13Security

We use encrypted connections, short-lived signed sign-in tokens and access controls. Only our operators can read the database. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your wallet and phone safe.

14International transfers

Our providers may process your data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect it.

1518+ only

CT Life is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor is using CT Life, email immadominion@gmail.com and we will close the account.

16This website

  • ctlife.fun uses no cookies, no advertising and no analytics.

  • To show the live Solana price, your browser asks CoinGecko for it directly, so CoinGecko receives your IP address.
