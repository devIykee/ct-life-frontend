Credits

Game footage and captures

Every clip on the site is CT Life gameplay recorded on a Solana Seeker from October 2026 builds, cropped and colour-graded for the web. Timeline images, tribe badges and origin cards come from the game itself. Some captures show development data.

CT Island map

The map is drawn live from the world data the game builds the island from: terrain, coastline, roads, building blocks, districts and venues.

Community nods

The communities on the island are nods to the people who make up CT. Their names and marks belong to their owners. None of them are sponsors, partners or endorsements.

Market Mood

Like the game, Market Mood follows SOL's real 24 hour price change. Your browser asks the public CoinGecko API for it. It is not a currency and not advice.

Privacy

No analytics, accounts, wallets or payments. Your browser remembers which eggs you found and whether you paused motion, on your device only.
