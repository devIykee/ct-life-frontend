A few things to know

Before you
move in.

Can I play in the browser?+

This site is a guide to CT Life. The game is a native Android app being built for Solana Seeker, with wallet sign-in. There is no public browser demo.

Is this connected to X?+

CT Life is an independent parody of Crypto Twitter culture. There is no Sign in with X in the current plan, and in-game activities do not reward actions on X.

Does Game Cash become real money?+

No. Game Cash stays inside the simulation and cannot be withdrawn. Future wallet payments are separate features and are not available on this website.

What about calls, projects and the wider city?+

They remain part of the plan: voice and video, paid calls, city life, player projects, ads and competition. The full scope is still being built; the screenshots show a development slice.

Are these final characters?+

Everyday and Breeze are our first two authored looks. A broader cast, mix-and-match customization and more animation are still being built. The images here are character studies.
