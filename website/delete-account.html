Delete your account

How to delete your CT Life account, what is erased, what is kept, and what can never be deleted.

Last updated: 28 October 2026App: CT Life (com.ctlife.app)

Before you delete

  • Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no waiting period and no undo.
  • Your Cash and everything you got with it are lost, with no refund.
  • Anything in your own wallet, such as NFTs and tokens, stays yours. Deleting your CT Life account does not touch your wallet.
  • If you sign in again later with the same wallet, you start a new, empty account.

01Delete your account in the app

  1. Open CT Life and sign in.
  2. Open your in-game phone and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the warning and tap Continue.
  4. Tap Delete forever to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once, you are signed out on every device, and CT Life's data on your phone is wiped. Files you uploaded are erased from our storage within minutes.

02If you cannot use the app

Email immadominion@gmail.com or eokorie1911@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CT Life account" and the wallet address you sign in with. Your account is tied to that wallet, so we will ask you to prove you control it before we delete anything. We will confirm by email when it is done.

03What is erased

  • The link between your account and your wallet address, your .skr name and your Seeker token
  • Your character: alias, look, tribe and traits
  • Your creator profile and its images
  • Photos and models from character likeness, if you used it
  • A linked X (Twitter) account, if you linked one
  • Notification tokens, notification settings and notification history
  • Who you follow, who follows you, and your blocks
  • Ad images you uploaded but never booked
  • Your place in voice rooms
  • Your sign-in sessions

04What is replaced

  • Direct messages you wrote now read "[deleted]". The other person's own messages stay in their inbox.
  • Feed posts you wrote now read "[deleted]".
  • Where the game still needs a name, you appear as "Deleted player".

05What is kept, and why

Your account record is not removed. It is made anonymous: it keeps only a random ID and the date it was deleted, with no wallet address, name or contact details. These records stay linked to that anonymous ID:

Record Why we keep it
Cash history, purchases and payments Accounting, audits and fraud prevention
Game history: Casino rounds, trades, quests, season and progress Fairness checks and keeping the game's records consistent
Reports you made, and reports about you Safety: moderators still need the record
Ad bookings already paid for The booked ad runs until the end of its slot
Game statistics Balancing the game. Deleted automatically after 180 days.
The date and time of your deletion Proof that we did what you asked

Our server logs, which contain shortened IP addresses, are not edited. They are deleted when they expire.

06What deletion does not remove yet

Deleting your account does not currently remove these:

  • Group chat messages you wrote. They stay in those groups. To remove them, delete them one by one in the group before you delete your account.
  • Factory launch records. For each token you launched, we keep the launch record, including the wallet address that launched it.
  • Event check-ins. If you checked in at a real event, the record that you checked in, when, and whether you chose to appear on the attendee list stays, linked to your anonymous ID. Your location was never stored.

If you want any of these removed, email immadominion@gmail.com or eokorie1911@gmail.com before or after you delete your account, and include your wallet address.

07What can never be deleted

  • Anything on the blockchain. Your wallet address, your purchases, your trades and any tokens you launched are public on Solana forever. Nobody can delete them, including us.
  • Token details on IPFS. The name, ticker, image and description of any token you launched are published permanently and cannot be removed.
  • Copies other people made, such as screenshots, or messages they quoted.
