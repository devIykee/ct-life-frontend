## Settled CT Life decisions

- Exact headline: **Welcome to Crypto Twitter**.
- Exact main CTA: **Get the game**, with no decorative arrow. Availability must remain honest; no invented public download.
- Retain the approved glossy CT mark intact. Use thick display type and a black, white and Solana-green foundation.
- No em dashes, developer-status badges, fictitious activity counters or decorative technical readouts.
- The reference supplies composition and hierarchy, not its artwork, trademarks, proprietary font files, copy or code. CT Life's original urban/social scenes must explain its own game.

## Composition mapping

| Reference principle | CT Life adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Large image stage, vertical identity rail, attached solid information block | An original urban or studio moment occupies the main stage; CT LIFE provides the identity rail; the exact headline is a typographic landmark; a concise attached block explains the multiplayer life game. |
| A sudden page-wide typographic statement after the opening | Introduce concrete things players do, rather than repeating the hero promise. Thick type establishes the change of pace. |
| World imagery and offset rectangular editorial slabs | Give the city, work and social rooms their own visual chapters. Use flat joined edges and strong alignments rather than a sequence of floating rounded cards. |
| One large changing-image chapter sequence | A small number of purposeful scene changes shows CT activities. Motion should change what the visitor understands, with ordinary scroll and a readable static alternative. |
| Purposeful mobile reordering and a ruled footer | CT Life omits the desktop identity rail on mobile and retains the horizontal glossy-mark/CT LIFE lockup in the header. It does not reproduce the reference’s giant horizontal mobile wordmark. Descriptive panels enter normal flow and footer information becomes compact readable rows. |

## Five visual pass/fail checks

These are review criteria, not claimed passes. Screenshots and actual interaction must support the eventual result.

1. **Opening composition.** Pass when the image stage, identity element and attached explanation form one composition, with the exact headline dominant. Fail if the result is another heading-left/image-right marketing hero with only its palette changed. Compare the desktop opening directly with the reference.
2. **Hierarchy and page rhythm.** Pass when the full scroll visibly changes scale and structure through opening, oversized statement, world/activity chapters and footer. Fail if repeated rounded panels or interchangeable section templates carry the page. Supporting activity copy must remain readable at its actual display size.
3. **Art tells CT Life's story.** Pass when the original scene shows a recognizable game activity or place and supports the adjacent words. Fail if a decorative friend ensemble substitutes for the activity, or polished concept art is presented as gameplay. Keep actual-build captures distinguishable without filling the page with engineering status.
4. **Mobile is recomposed.** Pass when the 390px view reorders the identity, image and explanation coherently, with readable text, reachable controls and no clipped content; confirm 320px as a boundary. Fail if it simply squeezes or crops the desktop composition, or overlays essential copy on an unreadable image area.
5. **Brief fidelity and purposeful motion.** Pass when the approved mark, exact headline, thick type, agreed palette and arrow-free CTA all survive, and observed motion has a clear scene or information purpose with a static alternative. Fail on a substituted slogan, remade logo, decorative motion everywhere, copied shooter imagery or invented product claims. Functional test counts alone cannot pass this check.

Reviewed `http://127.0.0.1:4178/reference-01.html` on 9 October at 1440×960 and 390×844 in separate isolated Chromium contexts, through the footer. All overlapping viewport captures were visually inspected. [Capture receipt](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/receipt.json), [desktop full scroll](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/desktop-full.jpg), [mobile full scroll](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/mobile-full.jpg). These are local website screenshots, not game captures. No code or art changes were made by this review.

Design judgment: the opening now adapts Marathon's actual composition through its large scene, vertical identity rail and attached green information slab. It is not only a palette change. The full-page rhythm is less complete: the extended typographic passage leads to one selectable capture section, then the FAQ and footer. The source reference's later world and activity scene progression has not been recreated in CT Life terms.

Three concrete corrections were sent to the implementer:

1. **Shorten the prolonged manifesto.** Its desktop 145svh section spends two overlapping viewport captures on the same statement and substantial empty space. The motion source translates/scales that statement and fades its note; it does not add a scene or activity. Reduce or unpin the passage until it contains a meaningful visual/information change. [Desktop evidence](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/desktop-02.jpg).
2. **Preserve the closing word boundary.** Desktop renders “SEE YOUON THE INSIDE.” because hiding its `<br>` also removes the only word boundary. Preserve a space independently of the line break. [Desktop evidence](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/desktop-04.jpg).
3. **Stretch the mobile scene tabs to the content width.** They start at x60 while the screenshot and information slab start at x20. The retained `align-self:end` creates a 40px gap, and “The side quest” wraps. Make this selector span the same 350px content width. [Mobile evidence](/Users/mac/Documents/codes/projects/ct-life-evidence/website/reference-20261009/research/ct-reference-20261009-world/candidate-review/mobile-02.jpg).

Copy correction: “Work a real shift” risks describing employment rather than a game activity. “Pick up a shift” is specific without that implication.

Rubric result for this snapshot: opening composition passes the visual criterion; page rhythm is partial; the studio scene communicates a recognizable CT activity and is labeled as concept art; mobile composition is partial pending the tab alignment correction; brief fidelity is present, but the purposeful-motion criterion remains partial. The exact headline, glossy mark, thick type, palette and arrow-free main CTA are visible. Image loading and absence of horizontal overflow at these two sizes were recorded, but they do not constitute design approval. 320px, pause/reduced-motion controls and modal lifecycle were not independently tested in this visual pass. No user approval is inferred.

## Final implementation and functional verification

The local default entry is now `website/index.html`, mirrored at `website/reference-01.html`. The rejected version is retained at `website/campaign.html`. No public deployment was made. The reviewed candidate includes the studio opening, shortened normal-flow statement, original morning city chapter, three real Seeker capture selections, FAQ, availability dialog and credits.

The reference supplied page composition; CT Life supplied original scenes, copy and identity. Original artwork and complete prompts are retained at:

- [Studio scene provenance](../../website/assets/reference-20261009/hero-space-provenance.json).
- [Morning city provenance](../../website/assets/reference-20261009/world-morning-provenance.json).

Both were made with the built-in image generation tool. Originals remain in the sibling evidence directory; only responsive WebP exports and provenance are in the website. The existing approved CT logo is unchanged.

Current motion uses event-driven animation frames. The statement settles as it enters, without pinning the scroll. The city image opens from a narrow inset to full bleed on desktop; mobile retains the full landscape composition. Pausing and system reduced motion restore the static layout.

Browser QA covered navigation, download information, Escape/focus return, gallery handoff, all scene selector keyboard paths, FAQ, no-JavaScript links, reduced motion and responsive containment. The first functional pass found two image-failure defects, both corrected: failed selection now updates content and accessible labels consistently, and initial load failure now exposes an explicit retry. Follow-up testing also found a shrinking mobile error frame; both loaded and failed dimensions now match at 320px and 390px. These findings were corrected before the local default was replaced.

Initial functional receipt and targeted retests are in the sibling evidence `qa/` directory. The normal-page checks reported no uncaught errors. Layout containment was also checked at 701, 768, 1024 and 1920px. These are Chromium lab results, not physical Seeker/Safari certification or user design acceptance.
