# CT Life landing-page roadmap

Updated 7 October 2026 · Website work only

**Status:** opening09 revises the hero premise to “Crypto Twitter turned into a game”, with DynaPuff display type, Chakra Petch supporting type, consistent headline scale and an animated Icons8 “Scroll Down” cue. Header navigation now contains “Get the game”; its destination remains pending. Original palette and character panels are retained. Follow [the current design note](website-current-direction-20261006.md) before applying older recommendations.

[Inference] The recommended website sequence is: explain the game, introduce recognizable people, show a day in their city, provide actual development evidence, then answer practical questions. The aim is a visitor who can explain CT Life and imagine something they would do there.

## Decisions and scope

- The user requested a first website draft followed by a concrete roadmap. Build and review the candidate before proposing another research-only checkpoint.
- The current hero uses the selected glossy green T and cyan/purple C with a transparent background beside CT LIFE. Its earlier background trials remain archived.
- The restored hero contains three original character concepts emerging above simple colored panels. Its original day-in-the-city story is the second section. The initial café ensemble was rejected; the seven-tribe selector, development gallery and FAQ remain earlier-draft material pending separate review.
- The game is a 3D multiplayer Crypto Twitter life-sim in development for Solana Seeker. Planned gameplay includes work, fictional markets, projects, voice rooms, homes and social status. The site must distinguish that intended experience from demonstrated functionality.
- This is a **website roadmap**. The game's six phases, city/identity, money/markets, voice/calls, city life, projects/ads and competition, remain intact. No game completion dates or scope cuts are implied here.
- Draft 01 is a separate local candidate. Publication remains a later action requiring the user's approval of the concrete site and destination.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] The user has approved the final candidate and the specific publication destination.
- [ ] All claims match current evidence. Multiplayer, calls, wallet authentication, payments, ORE staking and SKR spending are not described as working without end-to-end acceptance. A roadmap item is not a delivered feature.
- [ ] Game Cash is identified as fictional and nonwithdrawable wherever the market story could otherwise imply financial returns. Planned wallet features are explained separately.
- [ ] ORE and SKR remain represented in the intended scope, without fabricated availability, yield, prices or successful transactions.
- [ ] No unsupported release date, player count, testimonial, store badge, “free” offer, downloadable build or CTA has been added.
- [ ] Every screenshot/video has a source, build context and truthful caption; all concept art is distinguishable from captured gameplay.
- [ ] Asset provenance is retained: original/generated source and derivative exports, reference URLs, author/license information for third-party assets, and modification/attribution notices where applicable. The existing asset manifest and bundled licenses remain available.
- [ ] Visual references remain references. Their illustrations, logos, character likenesses and screenshots have not been copied into the product without the required permission.
- [ ] The approved logo concept has final source/export records. Any outstanding name/mark clearance question is stated accurately; visual approval is not represented as legal clearance.
- [ ] Desktop/mobile interactions, keyboard behavior, contrast, reduced motion, link destinations, performance measurements and final sharing preview have been checked on the candidate being published.
- [ ] Any form or external service added for the CTA has a verified destination, accurate data-use explanation and a tested completion/error path.
