# Solana symbol studies for CT Life
6 October 2026 — research and exploratory artwork; no approved identity or release asset.

[Inference] This document contains design judgments. Observations, user decisions and proposed artwork are separated below. Neither artwork has been audience-tested or validated as a small app icon.

## User correction and scope

The user rejected repeatedly forcing CT Life icon concepts into a face or mascot. They requested research into previous Solana logos and variations, specifically the campaign that sold spaces within the logo, followed by detailed artwork. This supersedes the earlier recommendation to lead with an anonymous mascot, and the older CT-lettermark recommendation.

The research distinguishes the core logo, historical collateral, temporary art treatments, event identities and unselected design proposals. No complete official archive of every former Solana avatar or logo treatment was found. This is a documented selection, not an exhaustive chronology.

## The fundraiser the user recalled

Final source verification: the original holographic-sticker and silver-bar X posts were both opened and visually inspected on the verified Solana account in Chrome. Their visible dates are 2 August and 12 August 2026. The silver treatment quotes a Sunrise announcement about physical silver coming to Solana; the material is connected to the post's subject. That announcement's custody and backing claims were not audited or relied on for this art study.

The official [announcement](https://x.com/solana/status/2094775606475124877) and [result](https://x.com/solana/status/2095173372158394780) were visible in an authenticated Chrome search of Solana's own account. The announcement image was visually inspected: three white bars divided by dashed lines into nine numbered spaces.

The [live organizer page](https://nepal.mallow.art/) states nine winners received placement in the official X profile image and pinned post for a week. It reports $166,946.50 raised and says proceeds were delivered, with transaction links. Transaction settlement was not independently audited here. The final visual was inspected directly on that site.

The top row contains Solami, 1win and SolARBa; the middle PreStocks, Rollbit and SOLCAT; the bottom Addog, MajorBots and SODAX. These are historical campaign observations, not brands to import into CT Life.

[Inference] The transferable idea is a stable outer mark carrying many different contributions. Merely copying the sponsor logos or adding visual noise would miss that idea.

## Scope of visual review

The [posts.design Solana collection](https://posts.design/brands/solana) lists 29 references. Its gallery was browsed visually from top to bottom; selected sticker, glass and fabrication references were opened in detail. These are archived social-post designs, not a verified history of all official profile pictures. Some treatments include characters; that does not make them appropriate for this user's brief.

Older official-hosted images and creator case-study images were downloaded to session scratch space and inspected. References were not bought, copied into production assets or passed off as CT Life originals.

Unverified claims excluded: a complete sequence of permanent logo redesign dates; specific Halloween/Christmas/clay PFP changes; unofficial fan redesigns being adopted by Solana.

## Artwork produced

Both images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using the official symbol only as a geometry reference. Exact prompts are saved beside the concepts. They are exploratory raster art, not gameplay screenshots or an approved production identity.

### Study 01 — CT lives here

[Inference] The three bars act as cutaway scenes connected by the same fictional project symbol: making a demo, discussing it in a Space, and contrasting late-night reactions. The outline is the first read, the social story the second. Generated people are illustrative; they are not approved game models or evidence of implemented gameplay.

[Artwork](../../art/branding/concepts/ct-life-solana-city-study-01-20261006.png)
[Exact prompt](../../art/branding/concepts/ct-life-solana-city-study-01-20261006.prompt.txt)

Observed output: the symbol is recognizable and the three scenes are populated. The renderer leaned toward photographic miniature scenes rather than the softer game style in the brief. Fine story detail will not survive small app-icon sizes.

### Study 02 — Resident Relic

[Inference] Nine inlaid sections borrow the auction's structure and the material studies' craft. Workbench/code, gathering space/microphone and home key/resident pass represent building, conversation and a life in the city. No people, faces or mascots are included.

[Artwork](../../art/branding/concepts/ct-life-solana-resident-relic-02-20261006.png)
[Exact prompt](../../art/branding/concepts/ct-life-solana-resident-relic-02-20261006.prompt.txt)

Observed output: three clear bars, nine visible sections, cyan/mint/violet materials, miniature architectural relief, copper/metal detail, a key and a holographic resident pass. It is still detailed brand art. It has not been validated at app-icon sizes.

## Recommendation and practical boundary

[Inference] Study 02 is the more useful starting point for an emblem; Study 01 is the stronger narrative artwork reference. If either direction is chosen, test a deliberately simplified derivative at 32, 48 and 64 pixels before claiming it works as an app icon. Detailed illustration and the small icon can belong to one system without carrying equal detail.

The exact three-bar outline is Solana's mark. The [official brand page](https://solana.com/branding) says alterations/combinations require written permission and prohibits suggesting an unearned affiliation. These private exploratory treatments do not establish permission, independent trademark ownership or release readiness. No application, website, store listing or public profile was changed.

[Inference] Keep the richer material/narrative work as a visual direction to assess. A permanent CT Life identity still needs a decision on permitted Solana-mark use versus distinct CT Life geometry.
