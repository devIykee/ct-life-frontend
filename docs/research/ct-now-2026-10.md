## 0. The mood in one paragraph

CT in October 2026 is leveraged, jumpy and suddenly full of suits. The Fed **raised** rates to 4.00% on 16 Sep, its first hike in over three years, and the 10-year yield hit its highest level since 2007. Crypto still squeezed shorts: BTC went from about $80k to $87k and people posted "officially back in a bull market" and "last invitation to altseason". Then an oil shock on 6–7 Oct dropped BTC under $83k and wiped $360M of longs in 10 minutes. The big hack was Bitget, roughly $350–390M, blamed on North Korea. The big memecoin stories were a tip-a-celebrity token that briefly sent Elon money and a former president's son's coin that crashed 99% at launch. TOKEN2049 Singapore is on now, 7–8 Oct, and posters say it "feels like the board meeting" rather than the afterparty. Side events are down about 40% on last year. Solana's attention is on Breakpoint London, 15–17 Nov. [Inference] The tone: the suits arrived, but the casino never closed.

**Game hooks:**
- Market Mood should swing hard within a day, not drift. The real feed flips between "so back" and "so over" in 24–48 hours.
- Add a **Macro** layer above Market Mood: Fed hike, yield spike, oil shock. Each one turns the city red for an in-game day.
- Conference Week reads better as "corporate and half-empty" with one great rooftop party than as an endless rave.
