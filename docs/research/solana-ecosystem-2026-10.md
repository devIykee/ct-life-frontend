  - Do not change design or scale proportions
  - Do not recolor or reduce contrast
  - Do not use the mark as an image frame
  - Do not distort, skew or rotate
  - Do not claim or register the marks
  - Commercial use or anything implying a Solana connection, incl. merchandise, needs explicit written permission (operations@solana.foundation; press@solana.org for media)
- **How to use the colours in CT Life:**
  - Purple #9945FF: the identity colour. Use for UI chrome, the title wash and focus states; it is the 'this is Solana' signal.
  - Green #14F195: the energy accent. Use for success, positive PnL, confirm buttons, the end of the gradient. Do not use for losses.
  - Teal/cyan: only as a mid-gradient or event-style accent; [Unverified] as an official colour.
  - Do not put the real mark on top of the gradient with effects, and do not turn it into a character (the user rejected mascot-izing, see solana-symbol-history file).
