# CT Life: community easter-egg registry v2 (draft for review)

Date: 2026-10-08. Status: draft v2, replaces v1. No art is made before you trim this.

Principle (your decision): we REPRESENT communities with our own original drawings. We never copy a logo or exact mascot art. Risk = how close the painted egg comes to protected art: low is a generic object or colour; medium evokes a protected mascot, so the drawing must be clearly our own; high would need the owner's permission (none left on the list).

Source rules: "yes" = cue read or seen today on a primary page (project site or its card image, CoinGecko, DefiLlama icon, brand page), on the 4 Sep 2026 @ivan_nomadz ecosystem map avatar, or in today's X findings. "partial" = part seen, part recall or the own site was not reached. [Unverified] = recall or inference. Source keys are in section 7; full URLs are in the JSON.

## 5. Title background brief (draft v2)

**Scene.** One sunny, uncluttered, hand-painted CT town square in bright 2D casual-mobile key art. A few big acting characters stand in the middle ground. Buildings and props frame the left and right edges. The **top centre is open sky** (flat gradient for the title). The **lower centre is open ground** (clear cobblestone for buttons). Each egg is a small prop, about 1 to 3 percent of the canvas, never a character, never in the two open zones.
