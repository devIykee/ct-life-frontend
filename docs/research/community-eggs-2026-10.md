Date: 2026-10-08. Status: draft. Nothing here is final art. You review and trim; no art is made before that.

Source rules: every cue below was read or seen today on a primary page (the project site or its social card image, CoinGecko, DefiLlama icon, brand kit or Wikipedia) unless marked. "Verified" means the cue was read or seen today. "partial" means part of the cue was seen and part is recall. "no" means nothing could be read. Anything starting with [Unverified] is recall or inference. Hex codes marked as sampled came from the official card image pixels. Sources are keyed in section 7; full URLs are in the JSON.

## 5. Title background brief (draft)

**Scene.** One sunny hand-painted Crypto Twitter town square, bright 2D casual-mobile key art. A few big acting characters in the middle ground carry the story. Buildings and props frame the left and right edges. The **top centre is open sky** (flat gradient for the title). The **lower centre is open ground** (clear cobblestone for buttons). Eggs stay small: each is a prop, about 1 to 3 percent of the canvas, none is a character, none sits in the open centre zones.

**Rules for the painter.** (1) A stranger must see a nice square first and the egg only on the second look. (2) No readable brand text except plain words we invented (ENTER, tips, faster, 402). (3) No real logos, mascot art or exact brand colour combinations except where this table says colour pair only. (4) Warm and cool balance: left side cooler (cafe, lavender-free), right side warmer (yellow, orange, green). (5) Max one saturated accent per area.

**Placement, left to right, foreground to background.** Each line: area, egg, note.

1. **left edge, foreground: Superteam Nigeria.** A yellow bus with a black stripe fills the far-left edge as a framing block. Purple door, purple-and-yellow bunting runs from its mirror up to a lamp-post. Only the nose and door are in frame, so the egg is the colour pair, not a sign.
2. **left, ground: ORE.** Pickaxe against the kerb on a 5x5 patch of copper-sheen paving tiles, with a wheelbarrow of ore. Sits at the lower left, outside the open centre ground.
3. **left, mid-ground (cafe): Jupiter.** Grey cat asleep on the cafe windowsill, collar tag painted with slanted lime-to-cyan bands. It is the warmest, softest detail in the left third.
4. **left, cafe table: Seeker (Solana Mobile).** Black three-lens phone propped face-down on the table, a coffee next to it.
5. **left, cafe chair: Backpack.** Red backpack hanging from the chair back. It is the strongest red on the left, so keep the rest of the left side cooler.
6. **left, under the table: dogwifhat (WIF).** Small tan dog in a thick rose-pink knit hat, very still, looking out at the viewer. A tiny acting beat, not a second hero.
7. **left, lamp-post: Phantom.** Ask first. If used: a child in a plain white sheet-ghost costume on the bench by the lamp-post, no lavender anywhere. If not cleared, drop it and nothing else changes.
8. **left, background: Colosseum.** Small arched round hall behind the cafe roofs, with a blackletter ENTER banner. It anchors the top-left skyline and leaves the top centre open.
9. **centre, mid-ground: Moo Deng (MOODENG).** Small brown hippo mid-splash in the fountain, mouth open. The fountain is the focal prop; the lower-centre ground in front stays clear cobblestone for buttons.
10. **top, whole width: Solana.** Sky is a soft purple (top) to warm teal-green (low) sunset wash. No logo, no bars. Keep the top centre as flat open gradient for the title.
11. **right, background: Popcat (POPCAT).** Brown-and-white cat in the bakery window, mouth a round O, ring doughnut on a string in front. One window, small.
12. **right, mid-ground: Claynosaurz.** Hand-modelled clay dino toy in the toyshop window, thumbprints showing. Next window along, a plain half-open book shop. Nothing else on this shop front.
13. **right, mid-ground (stall): BONK.** Yellow-and-orange striped awning on the market stall with a red exclamation-mark pennant and a mouthless dog balloon tied to a pole.
14. **right edge, foreground: pump.fun.** Curbside vending machine with a half-green, half-white capsule gumball behind the glass. This is the one saturated green on the right and frames the right edge. A single capsule on the pavement below it.

**Count check.** 14 Tier A eggs: Superteam Nigeria, ORE, Jupiter, Seeker, Backpack, dogwifhat, Phantom (optional), Colosseum, Moo Deng, Solana, Popcat, Claynosaurz, BONK, pump.fun.

**Where Tier B goes.** Loading screens: one B egg per screen, large and in context (Jito tip jar, Orca fish sign, Marinade bull, Meteora comet, Helius neon). Venue posters: Breakpoint badge on the Stage/Spaces poster, Axiom triangle and GMGN frog in the Trenches poster.
