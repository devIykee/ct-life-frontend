# Playtest fixes, 30 Oct 2026 (v1.3 device playtest)

Source: the player's screenshot and screen recording on a v1.3 build, plus Railway HTTP and realtime logs from the
same session. None of this was compiled here (no Unity import on the machine that made it); open in Unity, run the
EditMode tests and check on device before shipping.

## Game (Immadominion/ct-life #3, #4; mirrored on devIykee/ct-life-frontend)

| Report | Cause | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Apps stuck on "Coming soon" | Fixed separately upstream (server `livePhase` kept from GET /config) | `flags` is now kept too; staging build defaults to phase 6 |
| "Use terminal" and seat buttons did nothing | The walk had to end within 0.4 m of the approach point; furniture stopped it short and the action quietly failed | A walk that stalls within 1.6 m engages (`InteractionController.ArriveSlack`); sitting at a terminal opens Pulse |
| Chat did nothing on the street or at home | The chat bar only used the venue room | Falls back to the district street room; street lines reach the history with names; Send says when offline |
| Info boxes too small | 32 px toasts for 3.5 s on a 1:1 UI scale | Toasts about 35% larger and 5 s; prompt labels larger |
| Players look slanted | Mocap v3 jog leans 15-17 deg forward, sprint 19-23 deg forward and 6-7 deg sideways, plus a +10 deg code lean | `AvatarView.ApplyPosture`: low-passed spine direction kept upright sideways and 2-8 deg forward by speed; head keeps 60% of the change |
| "Get closer to the car" next to a parked car | Server stored a stale pose for the parked car (dropped moves) | Client retries once after `POSITION_UNKNOWN`/`VEHICLE_TOO_FAR`; server fix in ct-life-backend #64 |
| Wanted map pins and an arrow to follow | | `Waypoint`, map long-press / tap pin, Guide me on venue cards, minimap pin, `WaypointGuide` overhead arrow, clears on arrival |
| Apps look like white pages of text | Every app shared one iOS Settings look | Per-app themes (`duo-themes.uss`, `PhoneShell.SetAppTheme`); Wallet rebuilt as a crypto wallet (`DuoTokenWallet`) |
| Someone joined: show it | No presence message | Server `player_joined` (backend #67); HUD toast, friends called out, bursts summed after 4 s |

Performance: overlapping `PlayerProfile.Refresh` calls share requests (at most two GET /me per burst); Timeline draws
40 posts per page (was up to 200 rebuilt on every social change); an open DM thread draws the newest 80 bubbles
(was 300) and polls every 30 s while the city room pushes `dm`.

Tests added: `LivePhaseConfigTests` (frontend repo), `WaypointTests`; `WalletAppTests` updated for the new layout.

## Backend (devIykee/ct-life-backend)

- #64: vehicle entry radius 6 m, trailing parked pose corrected within 30 m, move bucket 20/12, refusals logged with distances.
- #67: `player_joined` from the city room (alias only, block pairs excluded, once per 10 min, rate limited).
- #69: market sim capped at a $1T market cap; a rising arc turns at the ceiling (fixed the `mcap_cents` bigint overflow flooding the logs).
- #62: GET /me in one query plus a parallel first-session read.
