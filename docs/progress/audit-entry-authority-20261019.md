## Change

`VehicleNetworkBridge` holds an acknowledged car kinematic until physical entry commits. It checks the live visit/channel and matching server driver for a pending claim as well as an active driver, and cancels a pending entry immediately on loss. A quick reconnection during a door wait therefore cannot revive that canceled entry. Abort callbacks release only their captured car; bridge teardown detaches a pending passenger before releasing an obsolete actor.

`VehicleSystem` keeps one captured entry owner and checks it after authorization, during the approach and seat interpolation, after the open-door wait and after the final close-door wait. Driver/control publication happens after the final check. Iterator completion, cancellation, disable and destruction share idempotent cleanup: stop approach movement, restore the last actual on-foot pose and parent, restore colliders/body controls, close the door and release the captured claim. Online entry without a live bridge/claim is denied; ordinary offline entry remains available. Entry also rechecks the existing OpenWorld, Stunned and Recovering state. A late acknowledgment cannot take control from knockdown or recovery; aborting after physical entry restores the other controller's body suspension. `LocalPlayerController.Init` creates VehicleSystem only for OpenWorld, and VenueSession assigns that flag from IsIsland; the explicit guard also covers leaving that mode before visit teardown.

The expanded fixture has **20 cases**, including the final close-door boundary, revocation without waiting for bridge Update, disable/destroy while seated, bridge teardown, reconnect during a canceled door wait, an unrelated stale abort, pending acknowledgment cancellation, delayed acknowledgment after knockdown/recovery, preserving that competing control during physical-entry abort, leaving OpenWorld mid-entry, normal local/online entry and actual six-metre-only floor availability.
